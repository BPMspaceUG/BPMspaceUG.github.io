2. Management Summary

The audit of «BCM 22301:2019 FND» closes with the verdict FAIL: 0 critical, 6 major, 15 minor finding(s) and 2 observation(s) across 6 chapter(s) and 33 question(s).

The Foundation syllabus BCM 22301:2019 is substantively sound but not consistently bilingual. The English text of chapter 503 is only a TBD, chapter 505 lacks the Disaster Recovery definition, and the situation definitions in chapter 504 differ in meaning between DE and EN. The topic description is not equivalent in DE and EN and the EN exam prerequisite is German. Of the 33 questions none has a wrong answer marking; one mistranslation (going concern) and typos were found. Chapter weights add up to 96.

Recommendation: Fix the six MAJOR findings first (EN chapters 503, 504, 505, topic description, EN exam prerequisite, translation of answer 7dfe4ecd), then typos and weights.
